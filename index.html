Our story

Quality you can taste in every visit

We roast, bake and laminate in house, so every cup and every crumb carries the same care.

Purpose

To elevate everyday moments through expertly roasted coffee, fresh bakery and crafted pastries, bringing people together around quality, flavour and genuine craftsmanship.

Vision

To become a leading destination for coffee, bakery and pastry, recognised for exceptional quality, distinctive taste and memorable experiences in every visit.

Mission

To consistently deliver high-quality coffee, bakery and pastries through expert craftsmanship, premium ingredients and attention to detail, creating a welcoming place that keeps people coming back.

هامِش

The name

In Arabic, hāmish is the margin of the page, the space where you pause and write what matters.

Hamish is that pause in your day: a good cup, something fresh from the oven, and a moment that feels like home.
