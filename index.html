Every cup, measured

Behind every cup at Hamish is a run of small decisions. Every step, every grind and every extraction is measured, so the coffee reaches you honest in taste and detail.

هامِش

In Arabic, hāmish is the margin of the page, the space where you pause and write what matters.

Hamish is more than a cup of coffee. It is the time when everything slows down, and the aroma that puts your mood back in order.

Purpose

To elevate everyday moments through expertly roasted coffee, bringing people together around quality, flavour and genuine craftsmanship.

Vision

To become a leading destination for coffee, recognised for exceptional quality, distinctive taste and memorable experiences in every visit.

Mission

To consistently deliver high-quality coffee through expert craftsmanship, premium ingredients and attention to detail, in a place that keeps people coming back.
